## Statistical evidence
- Gender mean spend difference is not statistically significant (p=0.734522).
- Education mean spend differences are not statistically significant (p=0.922359).
- Marital status and pet category are significantly associated (p=2.23897e-25).
- Age and DaysSinceLastInteraction have a near-zero, non-significant Pearson correlation (r=-0.003970, p=0.681691).
- State-wise mean spend differences are not statistically significant (p=0.345719).

## Decision-support takeaway
Use descriptive segmentation to generate hypotheses, but use statistical testing before presenting demographic or geographic differences as evidence of materially different customer spending behavior.
