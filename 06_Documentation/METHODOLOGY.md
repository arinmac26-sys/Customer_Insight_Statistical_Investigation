# Analytical Methodology

## Phase 1 — Descriptive statistics
Measure central tendency and dispersion for Age, MonthlySpend and DaysSinceLastInteraction.

## Phase 2 — Visualization
Use histograms, boxplots, categorical bar charts, scatter plots, KDE plots, crosstabs and a correlation heatmap.

## Phase 3 — Bivariate analysis
Evaluate relationships between customer attributes and MonthlySpend/activity.

## Phase 4 — Hypothesis testing
Use α = 0.05.

### Test 1 — Gender and MonthlySpend
Independent two-sample t-test.

### Test 2 — Education and MonthlySpend
One-way ANOVA.

### Test 3 — Marital status and pet ownership
Chi-square test of independence after creating PetCategory.

### Test 4 — Age and customer activity
Pearson correlation between Age and DaysSinceLastInteraction.

### Test 5 — State and MonthlySpend
One-way ANOVA.

## Interpretation principle
A p-value below 0.05 is treated as evidence against H0. Statistical significance should be distinguished from practical/business significance.
